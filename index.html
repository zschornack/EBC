ABOUT THE CLUB

More than learning English.

We work with English as a living form of communication — through literature, the formation of thought and real expression.

Our approach uses principles of Classical Education, including the Trivium and Quadrivium, combined with small groups and live online classes.

HOW IT WORKS

A semester built
around books.

01

Read

Students encounter complete works in English with support throughout the reading process.

02

Understand

Lessons explore vocabulary, comprehension, context and the ideas behind the story.

03

Discuss

Students participate in guided conversations and, for teens, structured discussions and debates.

04

Express

English becomes a tool for expressing ideas rather than simply memorising vocabulary.
